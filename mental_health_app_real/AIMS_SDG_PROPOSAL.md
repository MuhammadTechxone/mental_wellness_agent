## 1. Motivation
My interest in the AIMS SDGs Innovation Challenge stems from a commitment to leveraging mathematical sciences and "controlled intelligence" to solve the mental health crisis in Nigeria. While many AI solutions rely on "black-box" Large Language Models (LLMs), HeedX AI is built on a foundation of explainable Machine Learning (Support Vector Machines) and sentence-level NLP analysis.

Participating in this challenge allows me to:
*   **Scale Scientific Rigor:** Transition HeedX from a research-oriented framework to a production-ready intervention adhering to the highest standards of emotional safety.
*   **Address African Data Gaps:** Refine our "Nigerian Contextual Language Layer" to ensure mathematical models accurately interpret local idioms (e.g., "my head is hot").
*   **Contribute to SDG 3.4:** Provide a free, safe, and culturally adapted tool to reduce the burden on Nigeria’s overstretched psychiatric facilities.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
Nigeria faces a critical mental health gap. With 50 million citizens suffering from mental health issues, there is only **one psychiatrist for every 1 million people**. 

**Key Barriers:**
1.  **Safety vs. Empathy:** Current tools are either rigid rule-based bots or dangerous, uncontrolled LLMs that "hallucinate."
2.  **Linguistic Misalignment:** Global models fail to recognize Nigerian slang and indirect expressions of distress, leading to false negatives in crisis detection.
3.  **Stigma:** High social stigma prevents physical hospital visits, necessitating a private digital entry point.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution
HeedX AI is a hybrid platform that utilizes a **Linear Support Vector Machine (SVM)** for deterministic clinical analysis and **Llama 3** for empathetic guidance.

### (a) Key Features
*   **Sentence-Level Analysis:** Breaks narrations down to identify triggers for Anxiety, Depression, and Suicidal Ideation.
*   **Explainable AI (XAI):** Highlights specific sentences and "Confidence/Intensity" markers so users understand the logic behind the results.
*   **Crisis Routing:** Automated redirection to local resources (112, MANI, She Writes Woman).

### (b) Wireframes (Prototype Layout) link = https://muhammadtechxone-heedxai.hf.space
*   **(Home):** A minimal interface featuring a "Start Narration" button and immediate emergency hotline access.
*   **(The Wellness Narration):** A focused text area for users to share their stories, including a word-count motivator for better data quality.
*   **Screen 3 (Analysis Dashboard):** Visual bar charts showing the distribution of mental health indicators and a list of classified sentences.
*   **Screen 4 (HeedX Chat):** A context-aware chatbot that references the user's recent assessment to provide specific coping strategies.

### (d) Data Sources
*   **Labeled Clinical Narrations:** Curated datasets of mental health indicators.
*   **Nigerian Idiom Map:** A custom dictionary mapping local linguistic patterns to clinical indicators.
*   **Official Resource API:** Database of Federal Neuropsychiatric Hospitals (Yaba, Aro, etc.).

### (e) Challenges and Risks
*   **Data Privacy:** Mitigated by a session-based architecture where no PII is stored permanently.
*   **Clinical Safety:** Mitigated by deterministic SVM classification over probabilistic LLM generation for risk assessment.

### (f) Example of Application
A user writes: *"I feel like kpai-ing."* The SVM detects the localized term for death ("kpai"), flags it as high-risk Suicidal Ideation, and the system immediately triggers the MANI hotline overlay while explaining why that specific sentence caused the flag.

## 4. Value Proposition
HeedX AI provides **Explainable Transparency**. Unlike "Black Box" AI, we show users exactly *why* they are receiving a specific risk assessment. Combined with **Nigerian-specific cultural intelligence**, we offer a level of trust and local relevance that global competitors cannot match.

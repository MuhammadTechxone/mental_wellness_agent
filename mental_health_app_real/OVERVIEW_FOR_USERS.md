# 🧠 HeedX AI: A Safer Approach to Mental Wellness

## Welcome to HeedX AI
HeedX AI is a specialized tool designed to help you understand your emotional well-being within a Nigerian cultural context. Unlike general AI tools, HeedX is built with a specific "safety-first" architecture tailored for mental health support.

## Why Our Framework is Safer than Standard AI (LLMs)
Most popular AI today (like ChatGPT) uses **Large Language Models (LLMs)**. While powerful, LLMs have significant drawbacks for mental health:
1. **Bias and Hallucination**: LLMs can "make things up" or exhibit biases based on the vast, unvetted data they were trained on.
2. **Unpredictability**: It is difficult to guarantee exactly how an LLM will categorize a specific sentence.

### The HeedX Advantage: Classical Machine Learning
HeedX uses **Classical Machine Learning (Support Vector Machines)**. This approach is superior for this specific task because:

*   **Deterministic Accuracy**: Our model is trained on a strictly curated dataset of verified mental health indicators. It doesn't "guess" based on internet trends; it classifies based on clinical patterns.
*   **Reduced Bias**: By using a specialized, smaller, and highly relevant dataset, we eliminate the noise and cultural biases often found in massive global AI models.
*   **Sentence-Level Transparency**: HeedX breaks your responses down sentence by sentence. You can see exactly which thought triggered a specific indicator, providing a level of transparency that "Black Box" LLMs cannot match.

## The "Logic" Behind the Analysis
The system doesn't just give you a single "score." It uses an **Aggregation Logic**:
*   It looks at every single thing you write individually.
*   It identifies mixed states (e.g., you might feel "Normal" about work but show "Anxiety" regarding family).
*   It combines these specific insights to give you a balanced, realistic view of your current state.

## Privacy and Development
Currently, this system is in **active development**. Your data remains strictly within your browser session. We are using this testing phase to fine-tune the "intelligence" of the system to ensure it provides the most accurate Nigerian-context support possible.

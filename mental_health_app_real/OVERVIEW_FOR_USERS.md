# 🇳🇬 HeedX AI: Evidence-Grounded Mental Wellness for Nigeria

## Welcome to HeedX AI

HeedX AI is a mental wellness intelligence platform designed specifically for Nigeria. It combines advanced natural language understanding with Nigerian cultural values to provide supportive, evidence-informed guidance.

Unlike generic mental health apps, HeedX:
- Understands Nigerian English, idioms, and cultural context
- Prioritizes safety with dedicated crisis protocols
- Grounds guidance in clinical evidence and research
- Respects community, family, and spiritual resources
- Never makes clinical diagnoses
- Explains its reasoning

---

## Two Ways to Use HeedX

### Mode A: Professional Case Analysis
For psychologists, psychiatrists, counsellors, researchers, and case officers.

Upload or paste a patient/client observation. HeedX will:
1. Extract structured clinical dimensions
2. Retrieve relevant Nigerian clinical evidence
3. Generate a case formulation for professional review
4. Present findings without synthetic percentages or fake diagnoses

### Mode B: Conversational Support
For anyone seeking supportive conversation.

Chat naturally with HeedX. It will:
1. Listen and understand what you're experiencing
2. Remember context across the conversation
3. Offer supportive, evidence-informed perspectives
4. Suggest practical coping strategies
5. Recognize crisis situations and connect the user to immediate help

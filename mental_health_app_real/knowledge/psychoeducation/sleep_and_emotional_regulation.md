# Psychoeducation: Sleep Restoration and Cognitive De-escalation

## The Sleep-Mood Feedback Loop
Sleep disturbance is one of the earliest and most sensitive indicators of emotional strain. Chronic insomnia exacerbates emotional reactivity, impairs prefrontal cognitive control, and diminishes resilience to daily stressors.

## Evidence-Based Interventions
1. **Stimulus Control**: Reserve the bed exclusively for sleep. If awake for more than 20 minutes with racing thoughts, move to a dim room and engage in quiet breathing until drowsy.
2. **Cognitive Offloading ("Brain Dump")**: Write down unresolved concerns, examination dates, or financial anxieties on paper 1 hour before bedtime to disengage active night-time rumination.
3. **Paced Breathing (Box Breathing / 4-7-8)**: Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale slowly through pursed lips for 6-8 counts. This activates parasympathetic tone and downregulates autonomic tachycardia.
